Privacy Policy

SortFlow Collection Merchandiser · Effective Date: November 5, 2026

SortFlow Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting merchant and customer data. This policy outlines how information is handled when you install and use SortFlow Collection Merchandiser on your Shopify store.

1. Information We Collect

SortFlow Pro is designed as a focused merchandising utility. We collect only the minimum technical data necessary to provide collection sorting functionality:

  • Shop Details: Store domain (.myshopify.com) and OAuth access token to communicate with the Shopify GraphQL API.
  • Catalog Metadata: Collection IDs, Product IDs, inventory availability counts, and merchant-defined display positions.

2. What We Do NOT Collect (Zero Personal Customer Data)

SortFlow Pro does NOT collect, store, track, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) of your end-customers. We do not access customer names, emails, physical addresses, IP addresses, credit cards, or order histories.

3. How We Use Information

  • To calculate and synchronize manual product display order in your Shopify collections.
  • To automatically demote out-of-stock items and restore their exact pre-OOS rank upon restocking (Restock Memory™).
  • To maintain collection snapshots for the 1-Click Undo feature.

4. Webhooks & GDPR Compliance

We comply fully with Shopify's mandatory GDPR compliance webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact). When an app uninstallation or shop redaction request is received, all stored tokens, rules, and snapshot data for that shop domain are purged from our database.
