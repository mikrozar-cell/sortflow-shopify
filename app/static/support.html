SortFlow Pro Help & Support

Welcome to SortFlow Pro support. We are here to ensure your collections are merchandised smoothly, out-of-stock items never hurt your conversion rates, and restocked heroes regain their prime spots immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my collection switch to MANUAL sort order?

Shopify storefronts only respect custom visual sorting when a collection's Sort By order is set to Manual. If your collection was set to "Best Selling" or "Newest", SortFlow Pro automatically updates it to Manual upon saving, ensuring your exact merchandising layout shows up on your online store.

How does Restock Memory™ work?

When an item sells out to 0 inventory, SortFlow Pro automatically demotes it to the bottom of the collection. Simultaneously, it records the item's previous in-stock rank. As soon as you restock the product, Restock Memory™ automatically returns it to its original position without requiring manual re-sorting.

Can I undo changes?

Yes! Every time you save a collection order, a secure snapshot is preserved. Simply click 1-Click Undo (Restore Original Order) at any time to restore the previous layout.
